The brief

A young D2C brand making incense from temple flower waste needed an Instagram presence that felt as considered as its product: devotional without looking dated, premium without losing warmth.

The thinking

  1. One colour per river. Each fragrance in the Sacred River Collection owns a colour and its river's vahana, so a product is recognisable from the grid before anyone reads a word.
  2. A habit, not a post. The Ramayana runs as a 10-slide series every Sunday and Thursday until Diwali. Each carousel ends where the next begins, so people come back for the next chapter.
  3. Scripture, in the original. Every Ramayana slide carries the Valmiki shloka in Sanskrit with an English retelling and sarga citation. Depth is what makes the brand credible.

The RAP Plus logo and the existing packs (cotton roll, zig-zag cotton, bandage) are the client's. Everything shown here is mine.

The brief

An MSME with 100+ dealers across North India was launching six new products and taking a stall at Medicall 2026 in Bharat Mandapam. It needed to look like an established manufacturer to hospitals, distributors and white-label partners.

The thinking

  1. A pack anyone can scan in a second. White pack, navy band, lime wave, and outlined badges for Sterile and Class A Medical Device. Every SKU follows the same grid.
  2. Sell the factory, not just the product. The profile leads with numbers, certifications and the white-label offer, because B2B buyers are judging reliability.
  3. Two brands, one stall. RAP shared the stall with Surgi First. The walls give both identities equal weight while reading as one booth from across the hall.

The brief

A developer with 56+ projects delivered was marking 30 years and announcing a ₹17,000 crore growth plan. Its feed needed to carry corporate news, project launches and festival posts without looking like three different brands.

The thinking

  1. Sketch becomes reality. Every hero visual fades from architectural pencil sketch into a finished photograph. It is the developer's promise, shown in a single image.
  2. Numbers as headlines. On a faint blueprint grid with real dimension marks, big gold figures carry the story. Every stat is sourced on the slide.
  3. Locked rules, wide range. Cream ground, champagne gold, black headlines, fixed margins and counters. The same rules stretch from a ₹38,000 crore GDV slide to a Gandhi Jayanti post.
